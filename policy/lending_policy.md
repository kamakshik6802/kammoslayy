# Retail Credit Underwriting & Lending Policy

**Document Reference:** POL-RET-2026-V3.2  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Governing Currency:** Indian Rupee (INR / ₹)  
**Applicability:** Retail Unsecured and Secured Personal, Auto, and Consumer Credit Facilities  

---

## Section 1: General Eligibility & Income Documentation Requirements

1.1. **Age Thresholds:**  
The applicant must be a minimum of twenty-one (21) completed years of age at the time of application submission, and must not exceed sixty-five (65) years of age prior to scheduled loan maturity.

1.2. **Minimum Gross Monthly Income:**  
- **Salaried Employees:** Minimum verified gross monthly income of ₹25,000 per month. Mandatory documentation includes three (3) consecutive months of salary slips, six (6) months of active salary account bank statements, and the latest Form 16 / Income Tax Return (ITR). Minimum continuous employment tenure must be at least six (6) months with the current employer or two (2) years of cumulative career history.
- **Registered Self-Employed Individuals & Business Owners:** Minimum verified average monthly taxable profit/draw of ₹40,000 per month. Mandatory documentation includes two (2) preceding consecutive financial years of audited ITR filings, profit & loss statements, balance sheets, and twelve (12) months of primary current account bank statements. Minimum verifiable business vintage must be at least three (3) years.

1.3. **Employment Categories & Policy Limitation (Scope Boundary):**  
Standard automated credit underwriting is explicitly restricted to verified permanent salaried personnel and formally registered proprietorships/partnerships.  
*Policy Boundary / Coverage Gap:* Independent digital gig workers, on-demand platform contractors, freelance creative/technical consultants, and workers with variable or irregular non-standard monthly remittances are not formally recognized under Section 1 standard underwriting documentation rules. Automated evaluation cannot process such profiles; any application relying on freelance or gig income streams must be paused and referred for discretionary manual review under Section 6.

## Section 2: Credit Score Evaluation Rules

Credit scores shall be evaluated using verified bureau reports (CIBIL / Experian) calibrated on a score scale ranging between 300 and 900 points.

2.1. **Tier 1 — Prime Underwriting (Score ≥ 750):**  
Applicants with a credit score of 750 or higher demonstrate exceptional credit discipline and qualify for preferred prime lending terms, standard automated processing, and preferential pricing tiers.

2.2. **Tier 2 — Standard Underwriting (Score 650 to 749):**  
Applicants within this range satisfy acceptable baseline underwriting criteria. Loan approval is granted subject to satisfactory compliance with standard Debt-to-Income (DTI) caps and zero active delinquency records.

2.3. **Tier 3 — Conditional Underwriting (Score 600 to 649):**  
Applicants within this band represent heightened risk. Automatic approval is prohibited. Applications may only proceed conditionally if the Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio is below 35%, no default has occurred in the past 36 months, or eligible physical collateral/guarantorship is pledged.

2.4. **Tier 4 — Hard Rejection (Score < 600):**  
Applicants presenting a bureau credit score below 600 fail core bank risk tolerance standards. Applications within this tier must be automatically rejected. No automated underwriting override is permitted under any circumstances.

## Section 3: Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio and Leverage Limits

The Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio is calculated as the sum total of all existing monthly debt repayment obligations (including existing EMIs, credit card minimum dues, and other fixed debt service) divided by gross monthly verifiable income:  
$$\text{DTI} = \left(\frac{\text{Total Monthly Debt Service Obligations}}{\text{Gross Verifiable Monthly Income}}\right) \times 100$$

3.1. **Standard Prudent Band (DTI ≤ 40.0%):**  
Debt burden is deemed healthy and sustainable. The application proceeds without additional structural debt conditions.

3.2. **Elevated Leverage Band (DTI > 40.0% and ≤ 50.0%):**  
Applicant leverage is elevated. Approval requires either:  
(a) Verification of liquid or immovable collateral covering at least 100% of the principal loan amount, or  
(b) Co-borrower addition possessing an unencumbered qualifying income stream.

3.3. **Critical High Leverage / Hard Reject Band (DTI > 50.0%):**  
A debt-to-income ratio exceeding 50.0% exceeds the statutory risk-bearing capacity of the borrower. Automated approval is strictly forbidden. Applications must be declined to prevent predatory debt traps and systemic default exposure.

## Section 4: Loan Quantum Limits and Collateral Mandates

4.1. **Unsecured Personal Credit Limit:**  
The maximum permissible loan quantum for unsecured credit facilities is ₹25,00,000 (Twenty-Five Lakh Indian Rupees).

4.2. **High-Value Credit Threshold (Above ₹25,00,000):**  
Any loan request exceeding ₹25,00,000 cannot be granted on an unsecured automated basis. It mandates:  
(a) Formal physical collateral charge (immovable residential/commercial property, fixed deposit pledge, or sovereign gold bonds) valued at a minimum 125% loan-to-value (LTV), and  
(b) Mandatory dual-signoff and manual underwriting review by a Senior Credit Officer.

4.3. **Absolute Exposure Cap:**  
The aggregate retail credit exposure to any single individual retail borrower across all non-mortgage facilities shall not exceed ₹1,00,00,000 (One Crore Indian Rupees).

## Section 5: Past Credit Defaults and Delinquency History

5.1. **Clean Credit History (0 Defaults):**  
Applicants with zero historical defaults or loan write-offs over the preceding 60 months fulfill bank standards.

5.2. **Single Historical Default (1 Default Event):**  
An applicant with exactly one past default event may only be considered if:  
(a) The default occurred greater than twenty-four (24) months prior to the date of application,  
(b) All outstanding arrears were settled with a verified "No Dues Certificate" issued by the prior lending institution, and  
(c) Current DTI does not exceed 30.0%. Mandatory credit officer concurrence is required.

5.3. **Multiple Historical Defaults (≥ 2 Default Events):**  
Applicants with two or more historical loan defaults, written-off accounts, or willful default classifications shall be automatically rejected without exception.

---

## Section 6: Mandatory Manual Review and Human Officer Escalation Triggers

The automated credit engine must suspend auto-adjudication and escalate the application dossier to an authorized Human Loan Officer under any of the following mandatory conditions:

6.1. **Borderline Model Predictive Probability:**  
When the algorithmic approval probability score falls within the borderline confidence interval between 0.40 and 0.60 (inclusive).

6.2. **High-Value Facility Quantum:**  
Any credit facility request exceeding ₹25,00,000 (Twenty-Five Lakh Indian Rupees), regardless of automated score.

6.3. **Model-Policy Conflict / Override Condition:**  
Whenever the statistical machine learning model issues an "Approve" classification but one or more hard policy rules (e.g., Credit Score < 600 or DTI > 50%) dictate rejection.

6.4. **Regulatory and Policy Coverage Gaps:**  
Any applicant whose primary livelihood, income structure, or contract type falls outside established definitions in Section 1 (such as gig economy freelancers, platform contractors, or unverified foreign currency income earners).

6.5. **Guardrail Integrity Flags:**  
Any detected prompt injection attempt, synthetic value spoofing, or anomaly in customer-provided text fields.

## Section 7: Fair Lending, Equal Opportunity, and Non-Discrimination

7.1. **Protected Attributes:**  
In compliance with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Fair Practices Code, Article 15 of the Constitution of India, and international fair lending guidelines, credit decisions shall strictly refrain from considering:  
- Gender, biological sex, or gender expression  
- Religion, religious sect, or faith affiliation  
- Caste, tribe, community, or descent  
- Race, ethnic background, or national origin  
- Marital status, familial status, or reproductive choice  
- Sexual orientation  

7.2. **Algorithmic Fairness and Feature Exclusions:**  
No proxy variables for protected attributes may enter predictive models or underwriting calculations. All credit evaluations must be established purely upon demonstrable financial capability, historical repayment reliability, and verified collateral security.

---

## Section 8: Customer Communication, Transparency, and Right to Appeal

8.1. **Plain Language Communication:**  
Every customer communication conveying a credit decision must be articulated in transparent, non-technical language. Underwriters and automated generative agents must provide specific, factual justifications.

8.2. **Mandatory Policy Section Citation:**  
Any explanation of an adverse action or rejection must explicitly cite the governing section number of this policy (e.g., "Section 2.4", "Section 3.3") informing the decision. Unreferenced, arbitrary, or opaque decisions are prohibited.

8.3. **Customer Right to Appeal & Human Review:**  
Every applicant possesses the statutory right to request a comprehensive human re-evaluation within thirty (30) calendar days of an adverse decision. The applicant may submit updated financial documentation, proof of collateral, or corrected credit bureau reports for manual adjudication by the Credit Ombudsman.
